Mathematical tower

ℝⁿ → ℂ^(n/2)

Pair consecutive lag components into complex numbers. Hermitian metric H = g + iω recovers original L2 as its real part.

ℂ^(n/2) → ℍ^(n/4)

Pack four scalar coordinates (r,t,θ,φ) into one quaternion. Three imaginary parts yield three independent symplectic forms.

Killing-Yano

ω_I, ω_J, ω_K match the three Killing-Yano tensors Ω_I, Ω_J, Ω_K of Myers-Perry geometry (Frolov-Kubiznak 2007).
